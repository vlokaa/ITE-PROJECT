Letters to Marisol

by Ana Villareal

Synopsis

A mailman keeps delivering letters addressed to a woman who moved away years ago.

Chapter 1: The Wrong Address

Every Tuesday, Tomas found the same blue envelope at the bottom of his bag. The name read Marisol Reyes, a woman who had left Calle Mabini before he ever took the route. He told himself he would return it to the post office.

Instead he carried it to the old house and slid it under the door. The next Tuesday, a reply was waiting on the doorstep, written in careful handwriting: Thank you for noticing.
