The Empty Desk

by Gabriel Cruz

Synopsis

A teacher finds a note from a student who vanished weeks ago.

Chapter 1: Row Four, Seat Six

Ms. Alvarez noticed the desk had been polished. Nobody had sat there since Paolo disappeared, yet it gleamed under the afternoon light as if freshly cleaned.

Inside it, she found a folded paper with five words in pencil: Do not trust the principal. Behind her, the classroom door slowly closed.
