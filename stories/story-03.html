Seats Beside Me

by Carla Mendoza

Synopsis

A commuter and a stranger share the same bus seat every morning without saying a word.

Chapter 1: Route 14

Nina always took the window seat on the 7:10 bus. The man beside her wore headphones, read paperbacks, and never looked up. She had named him Mr. Chapter Nine.

One rainy morning, he handed her an umbrella and a book with a note inside: Page 214 reminded me of you. She read it twice before her stop.
