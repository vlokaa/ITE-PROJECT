The Man in Window Nine

by Chris Padilla

Synopsis

A train passenger realizes the same stranger has appeared in every photograph she took on the trip.

Chapter 1: Blurred Faces

Sofia scrolled through her vacation photos on the night train and froze. In the background of the first photo, a man in a grey coat was watching her.

He was in the second too, and in the third, each time closer. She slowly lifted her eyes to the window and saw his reflection, standing right behind her seat.
