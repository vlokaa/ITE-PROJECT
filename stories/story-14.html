Compass of Tidewater

by Noel Ramirez

Synopsis

Three siblings follow their grandfather's compass, which points to treasure instead of north.

Chapter 1: Needle Turns

Grandpa Isko's compass had been broken for as long as the kids could remember. The needle never pointed north. It always swung toward the sea.

On the first day of summer, Mika, Jun, and little Bea followed it down the shore, past the old lighthouse, to a rock with a red X scratched into its side.
