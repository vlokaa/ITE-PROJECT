The Great Sandwich Heist

by Marco Reyes

Synopsis

Someone keeps stealing lunches from the office fridge, and Dan is determined to catch the thief.

Chapter 1: Evidence in Mayonnaise

Dan set up a camera, a tripwire, and a very convincing fake sandwich. He told nobody. Not even Karen from accounting, who suspiciously asked about it twice.

The next morning, the fake sandwich was gone, and in its place was a polite note: Needs more mustard. Dan stared at it for a full minute before laughing.
