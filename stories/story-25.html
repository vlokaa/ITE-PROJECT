Neon Alley

by Zia Fernandez

Synopsis

In a city of rain and glowing signs, a courier delivers a package that begins to hum.

Chapter 1: Priority Delivery

Kai weaved her bike through the crowded alley, neon lights smearing across the wet pavement. The package on her back was small, warm, and getting warmer.

At the third stoplight, it began to hum. By the fifth, it spoke in a child's voice: Please don't deliver me to him.
