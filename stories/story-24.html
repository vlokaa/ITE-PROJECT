Where the River Bends

by Ramon Estrada

Synopsis

A teenager returns to his hometown to scatter his grandmother's ashes and finds an old promise.

Chapter 1: Homecoming

The bus dropped Migo at the edge of the river town at dusk. Nothing had changed except him. The bakery was still there. So was the crooked bridge.

In the urn bag, he found a folded map in his grandmother's handwriting. At the bend of the river, an X marked a spot with a single word: Promise.
