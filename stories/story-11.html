Signal from Kepler-9

by Arvin Dela Cruz

Synopsis

A radio technician on a lonely moon base receives a message from the future.

Chapter 1: Static

Commander Iris Vale had spent four hundred days alone at Outpost Lumen. Her only companion was a radio that played static. Then the static began to repeat in patterns.

She decoded the first line before dinner: Do not launch the supply ship on Friday. It was signed with her own name, and dated three years from now.
