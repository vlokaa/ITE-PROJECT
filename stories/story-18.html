Thirty Seconds to Midnight

by Vera Castillo

Synopsis

A bank analyst receives a countdown on her phone that no one else can see.

Chapter 1: Countdown

Alma was closing the last report when her phone buzzed. A timer appeared on the screen: 29:59:58. She swiped it away, but it returned.

At her desk, a shadow passed behind the glass wall. Then the timer turned red, and a message appeared underneath: Stop investigating Account 77.
