Fireflies in December

by Jules Mangubat

Synopsis

A boy believes the fireflies that appear in winter are messages from his late mother.

Chapter 1: One Small Light

Every December night, Teo sat on the porch and waited. The adults said fireflies did not live in winter, but they came anyway, one at a time, blinking softly.

He started to write down every pattern. Short, short, long. Short, long, short. One night, Aunt Ria sat beside him and whispered: Your mother loved Morse code.
