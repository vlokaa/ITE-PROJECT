The Dragon Who Hated Fire

by Joel Bautista

Synopsis

A young dragon refuses to breathe flames and sets out to find another way to be brave.

Chapter 1: Smoke Without Fire

Pyrax sneezed whenever he tried to roar, and his fire came out as soap bubbles. The elders of Mount Karn sighed. A dragon without flame, they said, was only a very large lizard.

So Pyrax packed three apples and flew down to the village below. There, a girl with a broken lantern looked up and asked if he could help her light the way home.
