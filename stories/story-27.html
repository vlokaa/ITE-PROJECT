The Quiet Knight

by Dante Rivera

Synopsis

A knight who never speaks defends a village that does not know his name.

Chapter 1: Silent Oath

He arrived with a dented helmet and a sword wrapped in cloth. He never said his name, but he repaired the village wall, one stone at a time, until the winter came.

When the raiders arrived, he stood alone at the gate. The villagers watched in awe as the Quiet Knight raised his sword, and the entire valley held its breath.
