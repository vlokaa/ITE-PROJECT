Do Not Open the Cellar

by Mara Lim

Synopsis

A family moves into a house where the previous owner left one strict warning.

Chapter 1: Rule Number One

The sticky note on the cellar door was faded but legible: Do not open. Papa laughed and said every old house had a quirk. Mama stuck a magnet over the note and forgot about it.

On the fourth night, young Pia heard knocking from below. Three slow taps, a pause, then three more. She counted. They were coming up the stairs.
