The Lamplighter of Intramuros

by Gino Aquino

Synopsis

In 1898 Manila, a young lamplighter becomes the messenger for a secret society.

Chapter 1: Flame and Fog

Every evening, Paco climbed his ladder and lit the lamps along Calle Real. No one noticed a boy with a taper, which was exactly why the revolutionaries chose him.

That night, he tucked a note inside the third lamp. A soldier passed, and Paco's heart pounded, but the man only tipped his hat and walked on into the fog.
