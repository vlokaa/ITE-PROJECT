The Salt Road

by Isabel Tan

Synopsis

A merchant's daughter crosses the desert in 1521 to prove her family's honor.

Chapter 1: Caravan at Dawn

The camels groaned as Inez checked the last knot on the salt bundles. Her father's debts were heavy, and the merchants of Cebu had given her one season to repay them.

She led the caravan out as the sun cracked the horizon. Behind her, the old traders whispered that a girl could never survive the Salt Road. Inez did not look back.
