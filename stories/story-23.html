The Piano in Room 12

by Hannah Gomez

Synopsis

A retired pianist moves into a care home and finds an old piano nobody can play.

Chapter 1: Out of Tune

Mrs. Dizon had not touched a keyboard in thirty years. The piano in Room 12 was dusty, half the keys stuck, and the lid was warped from the humidity.

Still, she sat down. Her fingers remembered a song before her mind did, and slowly the whole corridor fell silent to listen.
