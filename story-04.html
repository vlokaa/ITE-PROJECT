Crown of Ashfall

by Mika Santos

Synopsis

A kitchen girl learns she is the last heir to a burned kingdom.

Chapter 1: The Ember Mark

Eira scrubbed pots in the castle of Vael until the night a glowing mark appeared on her wrist. The old cook locked the door and whispered that the mark belonged to the royal line.

Soldiers arrived before dawn. Eira slipped through the cellar with a loaf of bread and a rusty knife, and ran toward the ashen mountains where the old kingdom still burned.
