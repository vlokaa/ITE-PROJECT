Trail of the Lost Mountain

by Ben Soriano

Synopsis

A hiking club takes a shortcut and finds a mountain that is not on any map.

Chapter 1: Off the Path

The trail marker said Summit, 2 km. The group laughed when Dario pointed out that the trail was supposed to end at a lake. Fog rolled in anyway.

When it cleared, they stood before a peak no one had seen. Its slopes glittered like glass, and a single wooden door was set into the rock.
