Moonwell Academy

by Selene Park

Synopsis

On her first night at a school for spellcasters, Willa finds a door that is not on any map.

Chapter 1: The Thirteenth Door

Willa counted twelve doors in the north corridor on her tour. That night, there were thirteen. The extra door was silver, and it hummed softly like a kettle about to boil.

She pressed her palm to it. The hum stopped, the lock clicked, and a quiet voice from the other side said: We have been waiting for you to arrive.
