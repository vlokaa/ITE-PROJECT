My Cat Runs for Mayor

by Jessa Flores

Synopsis

When a stray cat accidentally wins a town poll, the whole village scrambles to make it official.

Chapter 1: Paws for Election

It started as a joke. Mang Tonyo wrote Whiskers on the poll slip because he disliked every candidate. Then forty other people did the same thing.

By lunchtime, the cat had more votes than the incumbent. By dinner, the mayor was begging Whiskers to concede with a sardine and a handshake.
