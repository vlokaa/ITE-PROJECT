About Pagely

Pagely is a storybook website made for the ITE 1 project. It works like a small reading platform: browse covers, open a story, and read it in a clean layout.

Features

  • Dark and light mode that remembers your choice
  • Like button on every story
  • Show or hide the synopsis
  • Reading progress bar
  • Search and genre filter in the Library
