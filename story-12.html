The Last Garden on Mars

by Tessa Villanueva

Synopsis

A botanist tries to grow the first tomato under a red sky.

Chapter 1: Seed Zero

Dr. Okoye pressed one seed into the Martian soil and covered it with blue-grey dust. The colony watched through the dome as if it were a rocket launch.

On the seventh day, a green curl pushed up through the ground. Someone began to cry. Someone else laughed. Nobody on Mars had seen anything so alive.
