The Mirror Shop

by Wren Castillo

Synopsis

A girl inherits an antique shop where every mirror shows a different version of her.

Chapter 1: Reflections for Sale

Ivy had only meant to sell the shop. But on her first night inside, she noticed the mirrors never matched. In one she wore a crown, in another she was crying, and in the smallest she was smiling at someone behind her.

She turned around. Nobody was there. When she looked back, the girl in the smallest mirror had stopped smiling and was pointing slowly at the door.
