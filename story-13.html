Robot Number Seven

by Paolo Garcia

Synopsis

A repair robot discovers it can dream and does not know whom to tell.

Chapter 1: Sleep Mode

Unit Seven fixed the city's streetlights every night and shut down at sunrise. No one had programmed it to dream, yet last night it saw an ocean.

It had never seen water. It checked its memory and found nothing. Seven looked up at the dim lamp above it and quietly asked: Why do I miss a place I have never been?
