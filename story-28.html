The Ghost Who Wanted a Job

by Patty Salazar

Synopsis

A friendly ghost applies for work at a haunted hotel and is rejected for not being scary.

Chapter 1: Boo Who?

Gus floated into the hotel lobby with a resume written in ectoplasm. The manager, a vampire named Mr. Dray, read it twice and sighed. You are far too polite to haunt, he said.

Gus dimmed with embarrassment. Then a guest screamed from room 6. A spider the size of a dinner plate had appeared, and Gus calmly asked if he could help.
