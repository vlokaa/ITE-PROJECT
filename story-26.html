Starlight Diner

by Ollie Mercado

Synopsis

A diner at the edge of space serves meals to travelers who have lost their way.

Chapter 1: Table for One Planet

The sign over the door flickered: OPEN ALL ORBITS. Inside, an octopus-like waiter set down a cup of coffee for a pilot who had run out of fuel and options.

Outside the window, comets drifted by like slow fireworks. The pilot smiled for the first time in weeks and asked what the soup of the day was.
