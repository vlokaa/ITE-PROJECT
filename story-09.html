The Whispering Hallway

by Eli Navarro

Synopsis

A night guard hears footsteps in a school that has been empty for years.

Chapter 1: Third Floor, 2 A.M.

Rey took the night shift at Santa Rita High because it paid well and nobody else wanted it. The first week was quiet. The second week, the hallway began to whisper.

On the third night he followed the voice to Room 307. Every chair was turned to face the door, and on the board, written in chalk, was his name.
