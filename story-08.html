Case File 404

by Dana Ocampo

Synopsis

A detective is handed a case that was officially closed twenty years ago.

Chapter 1: Not Found

Detective Ruiz opened the dusty folder and found only one page. The case number was 404, the victim's name was blacked out, and the date was tomorrow.

She checked the paper twice. Then the phone on her desk rang, and a calm voice said: You are late, Detective. We started without you.
